The HolyC Archive

This archive acts as a central library to find community made creations of TempleOS and HolyC. You can request to have your own project, or someone else's, archived here.


Foreword:

Ever since first booting the TempleOS operating system, I have been amazed by what a single man, Terry, achieved under what he believed was a direct command from God to build a 640x480 digital third temple. What started as casual curiosity quickly sparked into a deep appreciation for it's language, HolyC. It removes all the bloat of modern programming, offering an ecosystem that is beautifully minimal yet undeniably powerful.

What people are going to read is, "It's about a pathetic schizophrenic who made a crappy operating system." My perspective is, "God said I made His temple." - Terry A. Davis
