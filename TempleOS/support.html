Currently trapexit is maintaining templeos.org. The domain, the server, the website, and main archive.

This domain was entrusted to trapexit by the community after Terry's death due to being a neutral 3rd party. Having not been a member of any TOS subcommunity and having kept the main archive of Terry's works and media. For that reason there is no interest in transfering or selling the domain. The intent is for templeos.org to be a simple memorial to TAD and his work and kept largely unchanged from how it was when he owned it. Links to related material, acting as a simple portal, is all the change one should expect to see. If infighting between subcommunities spill over into complaints about what is presented on templeos.org then we reserve the right to remove *all* 3rd party links.

If you wish to contribute financially to the maintainence you can do so via methods found here. Please indicate that the donation is for TempleOS if possible. That said such donations are not necessary and the domain, site, etc. will be maintained regardless. Additionally, no donation will change or influence what is presented on the site.
